# Outreach Batch Plan

## Batch Size

Start with 50 manually vetted prospects. If reply quality and usage signals are good, scale to 100, then 150, then 200.

## Segments

- Up-and-coming YouTubers publishing consistently.
- Thumbnail designers who post client work.
- Small creator teams or editors.

## What To Measure

- Open/reply rate if your sending tool provides it.
- Clicks by UTM segment.
- Test creation rate.
- Voting link creation rate.
- Replies that mention willingness to pay.
- Objections around trust, realism, or storage.

## UTM Examples

- `?utm_source=cold_email&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=launch_batch_1&utm_segment=youtuber`
- `?utm_source=cold_email&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=launch_batch_1&utm_segment=thumbnail_designer`
- `?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch_post&utm_segment=creator`

## Suppression List

Keep a suppression list from day one. Do not contact anyone who opted out. Do not use deceptive personalization. Do not scrape emails with code. Fill the prospect CSV manually or from sources where outreach is appropriate.
