# Launch Post Variants

## Variant 1

I built ThumbBattle for creators who are tired of judging thumbnails as isolated PNGs.

Upload a few variants, add the video title, preview them in a realistic YouTube-style feed, then share a voting link before publishing.

It is not official YouTube traffic-split A/B testing. It is a fast pre-publish gut check in context.

Looking for creator and thumbnail designer feedback.

## Variant 2

Thumbnail feedback gets weird when everyone is staring at a giant image in Slack.

ThumbBattle shows thumbnail options at feed size, next to competing-looking videos, with the title and metadata around them.

The simple question: which one would you click?

MVP is live for feedback.

## Variant 3

New tiny launch: ThumbBattle.

For YouTubers and thumbnail designers:

- upload 2-4 thumbnail options
- preview desktop/mobile feed contexts
- share a voting link
- collect votes and comments

Positioning is intentionally narrow: preview before publishing, not full YouTube A/B testing.
